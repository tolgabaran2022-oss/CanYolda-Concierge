## App Purpose

CanYoldaşı ("Soul Companion") is a community-driven mobile app for reporting, tracking, and managing adoption listings for stray animals in Turkey. Users can report stray animals on a map, manage their pets, and create adoption listings.

## Features & Access Steps

### 1. Stray Animal Map (First Tab)
- Open the app → First tab (Map)
- Colored paw markers appear around Istanbul
- Tap a marker → Animal detail card opens
- Tap "Help" to mark the animal as fed
- Tap "+" in the top right to report a new stray (requires location + photo)

### 2. Adoption Listings (Second Tab)
- Browse animals available for adoption
- Tap a listing → View details, contact info, and "Send Message"
- Tap "+" to create a new listing

### 3. My Pets / Evcilim (Third Tab)
- The **first pet is always free**
- Tap "+" → Fill out the form → Save
- **2nd and subsequent pets require Evcilim Premium**
- With Premium: Documents, Medications, and AI Pet Assistant unlock

### 4. Evcilim Premium Purchase
- Navigate to an existing pet → "Documents" or "Medications" tab, OR
- Try to add a second pet with "+"
- The Evcilim Premium screen opens
- Choose Monthly or Annual → Tap "Get Premium"
- **Restore Purchases** button is at the bottom of the screen

### 5. Listing Boost (Featured Listings)
- Create an adoption listing → Open listing detail → "Boost Listing"
- Choose a boost package (24h, 3 days, 7 days)

### 6. Messaging
- Tap "Send Message" on any adoption listing
- View all conversations in the Messages tab

### 7. Account & Settings (Fourth Tab)
- Edit profile, change password
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links
- **Delete Account**: Settings → Delete Account → Password confirmation

## Premium Feature Access

To test the second pet gate:
1. Sign in with the demo account
2. Navigate to the Pets (Evcilim) tab (1 pet already exists)
3. Tap the "+" button
4. The Evcilim Premium modal opens automatically

To test Documents/Medications gate:
1. Tap the existing pet
2. Navigate to "Documents" or "Medications" tab
3. The Premium-required screen appears

## Location & Camera Usage

- **Location:** Used when reporting stray animals or displaying the user's position on the map. If denied, the app continues to function with manual location entry.
- **Camera:** Used to photograph animals, update profile photos, or upload pet documents. If denied, gallery selection is available.
- **Photo Library:** Used for the same purposes as camera.

---

## Restore Purchases

- Tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the Evcilim Premium screen
- Any prior subscription with the same Apple ID will be restored

---

## Account Deletion Path

1. Bottom tab bar → Account (fourth tab)
2. Scroll down → "Delete Account"
3. Enter your password → Confirm
4. All user data is permanently deleted
