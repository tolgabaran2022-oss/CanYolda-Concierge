2. ACTIVE PROMOTION SOURCE OF TRUTH
==================================================

A listing is actively promoted only when:

promotedUntil is not null
AND
new Date(promotedUntil).getTime() > current server-compatible client time

Do not rely on:

isPromoted === true

Do not permanently cache an active state after expiration.

Create one shared helper such as:

isListingPromoted(promotedUntil)

Use it consistently.

6. REMAINING TIME FORMATTER
==================================================

Create one reusable formatter.

Recommended output rules:

More than or equal to 1 day:
“2 gün 4 saat kaldı”

Less than 1 day and more than or equal to 1 hour:
“5 saat 18 dakika kaldı”

Less than 1 hour:
“43 dakika kaldı”

Less than or equal to 0:
promotion is expired

Do not show negative time.

Do not show:

“-2 dakika”

Do not use vague labels when a real remaining timestamp exists.

==================================================
7. COUNTDOWN UPDATE STRATEGY
==================================================

Do not trigger a full application re-render every second.

Use an efficient update strategy.

Recommended:

- minute-level updates for remaining time labels
- recalculate when screen gains focus
- recalculate after app returns from background
- recalculate after promotion purchase success
- clean up timers on unmount

Do not create one unmanaged interval per card for large lists.

Prefer a shared current-time tick or parent-level minute update.

==================================================
8. AUTOMATIC EXPIRATION IN UI
==================================================

When promoted_until passes:

- remove “Öne Çıkan” badge automatically
- remove remaining time label
- listing becomes normal without app restart
- no negative countdown
- no stale promoted state

Since backend sorting already uses promoted_until, refresh relevant data when needed.

However, active-state rendering must also be derived locally from the timestamp so the badge disappears on time.
