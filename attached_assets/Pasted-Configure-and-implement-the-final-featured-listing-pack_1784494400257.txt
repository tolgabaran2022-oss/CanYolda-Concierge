Do not promise guaranteed views, guaranteed adoption, or a specific number of impressions.

2. STORE PRICE IS AUTHORITATIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do not hardcode `₺24,99`, `₺49,99`, or `₺89,99` as the final displayed purchase price in production UI.

These values are the required Turkish storefront configuration targets.

The application must display the real localized price returned by the native store through RevenueCat.

Use the RevenueCat package/product localized price string, equivalent to:

- `priceString`
- `localizedPriceString`
- Or the correct property available in the installed RevenueCat SDK

Adapt to the project’s actual RevenueCat API and installed version.

Expected behavior:

- Turkish App Store/Google Play account: Turkish lira
- United States storefront: USD
- European storefront: relevant local currency
- Other storefronts: their configured local currency

Do not:

- Convert currencies manually
- Use a live currency API
- Prefix every price with `$`
- Replace commas with decimal points manually
- Infer currency from the device language
- Infer currency from the user’s GPS location
- Show a hardcoded fallback price as if it were a real purchasable price

If the native product price cannot be loaded, show:

“Fiyat yüklenemedi”

and disable that package’s purchase action.
